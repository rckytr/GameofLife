Welcome to Conway's Game of Life

The Game of Life includes a of a grid of cells that change through generations based on a set of simple rules.

How It Works:

  • Still Life: Static patterns that don't change from one generation to the next.
  • Oscillators: Patterns that are repetetive and repeat themselves over many generations.
  • Gliders (Spaceships): Patterns that move across the grid over time.
